About YDRF

Our mission to restore hope and purpose to the youth

Our Mission

To provide comprehensive rehabilitation programs that empower youth to overcome substance abuse and reintegrate into society as productive individuals.

Our Vision

A society where youth are free from drug dependency, equipped with life skills, and empowered to achieve their full potential.

Our Values

Compassion, Integrity, Empowerment, Community, and Sustainable Change guide all our programs and interactions.

Our Story

Founded in 2024 in Nairobi, YDRF began as a small community initiative by former addicts who had successfully rehabilitated. Recognizing the lack of youth-focused rehabilitation services, they committed to creating a safe space for young people struggling with substance abuse.

Today, YDRF has grown into a leading youth rehabilitation organization, offering evidence-based programs that address not just addiction but the underlying causes and aftermath.

Our Programs

Comprehensive solutions tailored for youth rehabilitation

Residential Rehabilitation

6-12 month intensive program providing medical support, counseling, life skills training, and education opportunities in a structured environment.

  • Medical detoxification
  • Individual counseling
  • Group therapy
  • Vocational training

Community Outreach

Preventive programs targeting at-risk youth through school visits, community events, and awareness campaigns about substance abuse.

  • School education programs
  • Community workshops
  • Peer mentorship
  • Family support groups
Learn More

Aftercare & Reintegration

Support system for graduates including job placement assistance, continued counseling, and alumni networks to ensure long-term success.

  • Job placement assistance
  • Continued counseling
  • Alumni support network
